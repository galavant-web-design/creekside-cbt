Jessica F. Magidson, PhD

CBT Practice

About

Dr. Magidson studied psychology at Dartmouth College, received her PhD in Clinical Psychology from University of Maryland, College Park, and completed her clinical internship and postdoctoral fellowship at Massachusetts General Hospital/Harvard Medical School. She received certification in nature-informed therapy from the Center for Nature Informed Therapy (CNIT) in Maryland. She is a licensed psychologist in Maryland and Colorado. Her clinical training has focused on cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) and mindfulness-based interventions to address symptoms of depression and anxiety, as well as adherence and self-care to promote healthy behavior change. She has experience collaborating with primary care physicians, psychiatrists, and other health providers. She has been trained in nature-informed therapy approaches and how to deliver evidence-based care outdoors.

She has a deep personal passion for nature and being in the outdoors. Starting as a child from fishing with her grandparents at the lake and hiking in the mountains in Idaho, she is now bringing her love for exploring the outdoors to her three young children (and clients!). She spends as much time as she can outside, on the trail, and near the creek. She is equally as passionate about supporting individuals through transitions and how we can live purposeful, meaningful lives with balance.

Credentials

Jessica F. Magidson, PhD is a Massachusetts General Hospital/Harvard-trained licensed clinical psychologist who specializes in cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) and mindfulness-based interventions to address symptoms of depression and anxiety. She is a licensed psychologist in Colorado and Maryland, certified in nature-informed therapy.

She is a tenured Associate Professor at University of Maryland, leading a Center conducting research to evaluate the implementation of CBT and mindfulness in a variety of clinical and research settings all over the world. She has published over 150 peer-reviewed journal articles and book chapters on this topic and received over $25 million from the National Institutes of Health and other funders to support her work over the past decade. She also conducts seminars and speaks at national and international conferences on the implementation of CBT and mindfulness-based interventions.

Her area of deep passion is the outdoors and nature-based delivery of evidence-based interventions. She also brings valued lived experience into her work through her passion for the outdoors and nature, navigating balance as a mom of three young children, building healthy daily routines including postpartum and return to work routines, and sharing in postpartum and fertility journeys.
